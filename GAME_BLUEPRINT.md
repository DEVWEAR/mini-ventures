# Mini Ventures — Creative Game Blueprint
Version 0.3 · October 2026

## Vision
An original, premium hand-painted 2D cartoon business adventure. Begin with a street-food cart, master delightful tactile cooking, build a neighborhood of distinct businesses, and watch a lively city react to player decisions. Never imitate characters, artwork, or UI from existing games.

## Visual identity
- Original expressive 2D characters with distinctive silhouettes, eyes, hairstyles, body proportions, outfits, facial expressions and emotion animations.
- Rich hand-painted interiors and food illustrations, warm lighting, layered depth, readable mobile-first interface.
- Consistent character turnarounds, animation sheets and UI art direction before production implementation.
- Avoid emoji and primitive SVG people as final production assets. Existing site is a gameplay placeholder only.
- Aim for crisp mobile rendering, accessibility and performance on iPhone Safari.

## Core loop
1. Customer arrives with a visible order and patience.
2. Player selects ingredients, cooks/prepares, plates, and serves through satisfying short interactions.
3. Quality, speed, customer personality and order accuracy determine tips, reviews, combo and reputation.
4. Reinvest profits into equipment, recipes, staff training, decor and business expansion.
5. Unlock new businesses by play, never by mandatory payment.

## Differentiating systems
- Living customer stories: regulars remember service, discover favorites, bring friends, and react to menu changes.
- Smart city demand: day/night, weather and local events alter menu demand and footfall, transparently and fairly.
- Mini venture network: café, bakery, flower shop, boutique and more each with unique mechanics, shared staff/finance decisions.
- Player-designed identity: store names, themes, décor, uniforms and storefront customization.
- Meaningful staffing: distinct strengths, morale, schedules and training rather than simple passive income.
- Neighborhood events and optional challenge scenarios with clear achievable goals.
- Daily quests and a relaxed no-pressure mode, with no punitive streak mechanics.
- Arabic RTL and English localization from day one, including culturally appropriate UI and text.
- Local saves first; cloud saves only with a transparent opt-in later.

## Release plan
Phase 1: Visual art bible, main hero and food cart concept, expression sheets, menu illustrations.
Phase 2: Real cooking-order mechanics, order queue, patience, scoring, and upgrades.
Phase 3: Hand-drawn animation pipeline, effects, polished sound, tutorials and accessibility.
Phase 4: City map, additional businesses, customer memory, staff progression.
Phase 5: QA, performance, balance, optional monetization, mobile packaging.

## Monetization
Free core game and unlockable businesses. Optional cosmetics and rewarded ads only, no forced ads or pay-to-win gates. No purchases in the browser prototype.

## Production guardrails
Do not call placeholders finished game art. Verify code and gameplay after every change. Preserve player progress when upgrading saves. Keep mobile interactions intuitive and performant.
